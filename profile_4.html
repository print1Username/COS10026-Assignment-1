Hometown Kuching, the capital city of Sarawak in Malaysia, is a charming and vibrant city with a rich cultural heritage, diverse cuisine, and unique architecture. The city is a mix of modern and traditional buildings, and its beautiful waterfront along the Sarawak River adds to its charm. Visitors can experience the culture of the various ethnic groups in Sarawak through traditional dances, music, and food at the Sarawak Cultural Village, and there are several museums and night markets to explore. Kuching is also known for its delicious dishes, including Sarawak Laksa and Kolo Mee, making it a must-visit destination for anyone traveling to Malaysia.
