Figure

Description:
HTML5 provides the 'figure' and 'figcaption' elements that allow for an image to be linked with a caption, making it easier to organize images and their descriptions as a cohesive unit without affecting the overall layout of the document. Although there may not be a significant visual difference, the use of these elements offers a way to semantically categorize the image and its corresponding caption. This ensures that the image and caption are viewed as a single, self-contained entity.

Citation:
https://www.w3schools.com/tags/tryit.asp?filename=tryhtml5_figure
